# Submission video script — target 4:30

Record at 1080p/30 fps. Hide notifications, account identifiers, secrets, and unrelated tabs. The
public demo has no broker capability. Use the final MP4 only after verifying duration under five
minutes and file size under 300 MB.

## 0:00–0:20 — Hook

**On screen:** Cover / slide 1, then dashboard title.

> Most trading agents try to prove why they should trade. We built the one that can prove why it
> refused. This is AxiomWeave AlphaLedger: an abstention-first, defined-risk options agent for
> Alpaca paper trading. Proof before profit.

## 0:20–0:50 — The problem

**On screen:** Problem slide.

> In options, a stale quote, missing Greek, wide market, wrong leg, or understated debit can change
> the risk completely. So our model is not the authority. AI may critique or veto, but only
> independently recomputed policy can permit a paper order.

## 0:50–1:25 — Competition controller

**On screen:** **Competition control** tab.

> The organizer window runs Monday August thirty-first at 9:30 Eastern through Friday September
> fourth at 9:30. One exclusive writer pins the exact fresh one-hundred-thousand-dollar Level 3
> paper account. It allows one SPY spread attempt per day, risks at most two hundred fifty dollars,
> stops at five hundred dollars daily or one thousand total drawdown, and forces flat Thursday at
> 3:45. Alpaca confirmed judges use total equity as of Thursday EOD and that Thursday-expiry
> exercise and assignment are reflected. We flatten first to keep the scored state deterministic.

> We also tested two bounded signal families. Neither passed its held-out promotion gate. The
> dashboard says promotion refused, because local backtests are not proof of future profit.

## 1:25–2:15 — Valid defined-risk path

**On screen:** **Options agent** → **Valid defined-risk spread** → run.

> The proposer builds a same-expiration one-to-one debit spread. The gate recomputes the debit and
> maximum loss from executable quote sides, then checks paper mode, freshness, feeds, Greeks,
> liquidity, evidence, buying power, drawdown, positions, and open orders. A valid structure becomes
> an Alpaca multi-leg plan. This public page cannot send it.

> In the private competition runner, the same decision boundary uses Alpaca's official CLI,
> deterministic client IDs, bounded cancellation and close retries, and status reconciliation
> before it considers the plan owned.

## 2:15–2:50 — One-variable falsifier

**On screen:** Change only to **Stale option evidence** → run.

> Now I change one fact: quote age. The outcome becomes abstain, the order plan disappears, and the
> exact stale-contract reason is recorded. Model confidence cannot bypass freshness. The same path
> rejects missing Greeks, wide markets, low open interest, account mismatch, a second writer, or a
> live endpoint.

## 2:50–3:20 — Evidence ledger

**On screen:** **Evidence ledger**.

> Passes and refusals share one SHA-256 hash chain. Each receipt binds the observation, proposal,
> policy, advisory, recomputed economics, account state, broker action, and previous hash. Change an
> earlier entry and verification fails.

## 3:20–3:55 — Evidence honesty

**On screen:** Evaluation / evidence slide.

> The verified suite establishes C1 deterministic mechanics on frozen and Alpaca-shaped inputs.
> Authenticated organizer screenshots establish C2 timing evidence. Profitability, future fills,
> broker mark construction, and competition rank remain C0. We preserve the failed hourly and five-minute
> tests rather than tuning them away.

## 3:55–4:20 — Business value

**On screen:** Product wedge / architecture slide.

> AlphaLedger is a decision-provenance layer for brokerages, fintech platforms, wealth tools, and
> agent teams. The immediate product is a transparent paper agent; the durable wedge is a receipt
> for every action and refusal before autonomous finance scales.

## 4:20–4:30 — Close

**On screen:** Closing slide.

> Trust begins with the option trade an agent refuses. We are AxiomWeave AlphaLedger. Give us a
> falsifier.
