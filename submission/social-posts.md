## LinkedIn

We built **AxiomWeave AlphaLedger** for the Alpaca AI Trading Agents Hackathon.

The design question was simple: what would make an autonomous options decision inspectable before
we trusted it? Our answer is an abstention-first agent that proposes capped-loss SPY debit spreads,
lets AI critique or veto but never authorize, independently recomputes risk from quote evidence, and
writes every action or refusal to a hash-linked receipt.

The uncomfortable result is part of the project: two bounded historical signal searches did not
clear their frozen held-out promotion gates. We kept that evidence visible. The competition route is
therefore C0 exploratory, backed by C1 deterministic mechanics—not a profitability promise.

## Discord project showcase

**AxiomWeave AlphaLedger — Proof before profit**

An abstention-first Alpaca paper-options agent. It builds same-expiration defined-risk SPY spreads,
recomputes risk independently, lets AI veto but never authorize, and records every trade/refusal in
a hash-linked receipt. Judges can flip one fact—freshness, Greeks, liquidity, loss, or account
state—and watch it fail closed.
